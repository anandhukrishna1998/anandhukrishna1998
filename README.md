## About me

I'm an AI engineer and data scientist based in Paris. My work is mostly around LLM systems, RAG pipelines, and the infrastructure needed to make them usable in production.

Before working on applied AI in France, I spent three years building ML systems in telecom — churn prediction, recommendation engines, and forecasting models. Most of what I enjoy sits in the gap between research ideas and systems that actually hold up in real use.

---

## What I work on

- 🔧 LLM pipelines, evaluation, observability, and prompt reliability
- 📄 Unstructured data systems for contracts, voice, email, and text
- 🔒 Prompt injection detection, guardrails, and privacy-aware pipelines
- ⚙️ Production ML and AI services with Docker, FastAPI, CI/CD, and GCP

---

## Recent work

**IPSOS BVA** *(Paris)*  
Built AI services for a quality monitoring platform used across chat, email, and voice. Worked on model-agnostic design, MCP-based RAG, prompt injection detection, and secure processing pipelines.

**AXA Group Operations** *(Paris)*  
Worked on structuring complex insurance contracts into usable data. Evaluated more than 25 parsing approaches, built around multimodal LLM workflows, and used Neo4j and Kuzu for knowledge graph and semantic retrieval work.

**6D Technologies** *(India)*  
Three years as a data scientist building telecom ML systems — churn prediction (−15% attrition), product recommendation (engagement +25%, revenue +10%), and next-purchase forecasting using XGBoost with RFM features at 62% accuracy.
